Research date: September 18, 2026. These are discovered official starting sources, not a completed legal review or approved app content. Search indexing/retrieval dates are not statutory effective dates. Verify each source's text, amendments, effective dates, exceptions and relevant court orders before drafting guidance. No production rules are published by this document.

## Federal and adjacent jurisdictions

[ATF's state-law index](https://www.atf.gov/firearms/tools-and-services-firearms-industry/state-laws-and-published-ordinances-firearms?page=1) provides routes to official material; a compilation is not proof that every amendment or court order is included. Prefer the actual current statute for the final citation.

[18 USC 926A](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section926A&num=0&edition=prelim) requires a separate transport analysis. Permit recognition alone does not establish its prerequisites. Review other applicable federal restrictions independently. Pennsylvania, DC or other states on an actual route cannot be ignored because they are outside the selected coastal content scope.

## Research method that reduces later review effort

1. Separate questions: possession, open/concealed carry, permit recognition, transport, restricted places, magazines/ammunition, notification duties, and special firearm categories. A source answering one does not answer all.
2. For each proposed summary, record exact statutory section, official URL, source/version date, effective date, date checked, material conditions and exceptions, and any unresolved court-order issue. Paraphrase rather than copying commercial guides.
3. Cross-check agency guidance against the underlying statute. Conflicts or missing facts remain visible as unresolved; do not resolve them using an AI confidence score.
4. Show concise source-backed reference text and direct official links. Label the check as source verification, not lawyer review or guaranteed legal permission. Preserve unknown outcomes for incomplete profile matching.
5. Keep a review log and revisit sources when changes are discovered, with the existing 90-day expiry as an outer freshness limit, not a guarantee. Do not label content human-reviewed until someone actually performs and records that review.
6. If obtaining counsel later, request a defined scope and quote for the unresolved issues, matching logic and final terms/privacy wording. Source collection and a question list can make that engagement more focused; cost savings are not guaranteed.

## Privacy and terms research

The [FTC's app marketing/privacy guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/marketing-your-mobile-app-get-it-right-start) and [security guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/app-developers-start-security) are free primary resources. Match statements to actual app/provider behavior; a generic template cannot establish compliance. Existing Apple/Google policy sources are recorded in REVIEW.md.

The working contracts can be improved using these sources, but the missing support address, retention practices, audience/age policy and operator jurisdiction are business facts that web research cannot choose for Crossline. Disclaimers do not make inaccurate substantive guidance reliable or eliminate liability. The previous recommendation for qualified review is a risk-management recommendation, not a claim that every informational app is legally required to retain counsel.

## App implementation

The app now bundles `data/legal-references.json` with concise source descriptions and unresolved questions, displayed in the public `/references` library and the law browser. Trip cards link to their state reference. These are research notes, not rows in the reviewed `state_laws` or `carry_rules` tables. They never drive status colors. Notes are hidden after 90 days or for invalid/future research dates; official links remain available. Full-text retrieval failed for the primary MA, NJ and VA resources, and those entries explicitly disclose incomplete verification. Research dates do not stand in for effective dates. A later review must check current statutes, exceptions and orders before promoting anything into personalized guidance.
