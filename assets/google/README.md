# Google sign-in artwork

Unmodified official light, rectangular, text-inclusive PNG buttons from
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip.

The Android/Web and iOS images are the 4x assets, displayed at their intended
logical dimensions (180 × 40 and 188 × 44 respectively).

Branding guidance: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
